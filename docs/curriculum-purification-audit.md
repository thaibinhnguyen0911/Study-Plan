**Document Classification:** Academic Audit Artifact & Curricular Preservation Record  
**Target Path:** `docs/curriculum-purification-audit.md`  
**Governing Standard:** Primary-source-centered, historically grounded liberal arts education without mechanical drills, applied trade manuals, or editorial clutter.

---

## Executive Summary

This audit documents the systematic purification and intellectual reconstruction of the Liberal Arts curriculum across its 381 works and 204 verified book-to-book intellectual connections. In accordance with the governing philosophy of a Great Books liberal arts education, the curriculum privileges primary sources, intellectual history, philosophical inquiry, narrative explanations of the development of ideas, and substantive scholarly debates.

All modern formal drill manuals, standardized composition workbooks, and applied trade procedures have been systematically removed and replaced with world-renowned canonical primary sources and foundational philosophical companions. Every replacement has been integrated into the existing course hierarchy with zero disruption to the protected Music Conservatory (76 certifications) and Language (22 genealogical records) tracks.

### Detailed Analysis of Purged Works

#### 1. ORIG-035: *forall x: Calgary* → Aristotle's *Prior Analytics (Book I)* & Russell's *Introduction to Mathematical Philosophy*
* **Course Assignment:** MAT 205 (*Euclid and Formal Logic*, Stage 200)
* **Conflict with Curricular Vision:** *forall x: Calgary* is an open-source formal logic textbook designed for standard undergraduate problem-set courses. Its pedagogy relies on Bourbaki-style formal syntax, truth-table calculations, and natural deduction derivation exercises. It presents logical formalisms ahistorically, divorced from the philosophical crises that produced deductive rigor (e.g., the Eleatic paradoxes, Euclidean axiomatization, and the nineteenth-century foundational crisis).
* **Replacement Strategy:**
  1. *Primary Anchor (ORIG-035):* Aristotle's **Prior Analytics (Book I)**. The foundational primary text in human history that invented the deductive syllogism, formal term variables, and the theory of demonstrative science. It engages directly with Euclid's *Elements* (`ORIG-034`) and sets the classical baseline of deductive validity.
  2. *Narrative Companion (REPL-001):* Bertrand Russell's **Introduction to Mathematical Philosophy** (1919). Written with minimal non-essential symbolic clutter, Russell provides an unparalleled narrative exposition of the philosophical development from Peano arithmetic and Cantor's set theory through Frege's logicism to the paradoxes of self-reference.

#### 2. ORIG-008 & ORIG-009: Applied History Guides → Vico's *The New Science* & Collingwood's *The Idea of History*
* **Course Assignment:** HIS 102 (*The Historian's Craft*, Stage 100)
* **Conflict with Curricular Vision:** *Doing Oral History* and applied public history trade guides teach operational interviewing techniques, legal release forms, digital repository software, and project coordination. They treat history as an applied technical career rather than a humanistic mode of understanding human thought and civil institutions.
* **Replacement Strategy:**
  1. *Primary Anchor (ORIG-008):* Giambattista Vico's **The New Science** (1744). Vico establishes the philosophical autonomy of the human sciences through the revolutionary principle of *verum-factum*—that humans can truly understand the civil world because human thought created it.
  2. *Philosophical Companion (ORIG-009):* R. G. Collingwood's **The Idea of History** (1946). Collingwood traces the historical evolution of historiographical consciousness from Herodotus through the Enlightenment to the modern era, establishing that all history is the reenactment of past thought in the historian's own mind.

#### 3. ORIG-015: Composition Guide → Plato's *Gorgias*
* **Course Assignment:** PHI 102 (*Ethics and Philosophical Writing*, Stage 100)
* **Conflict with Curricular Vision:** Martinich's *Philosophical Writing* teaches formal essay structure, counterexample generation, and stylistic formatting. It abstracts the act of philosophical argumentation away from existential inquiry into truth and virtue.
* **Replacement Strategy:**
  - Plato's **Gorgias** (*ORIG-015*). The premier philosophical masterpiece that directly investigates what it means to speak, argue, and write philosophically. Socrates cross-examines Gorgias, Polus, and Callicles on whether persuasive speech aims at flattery or truth, confronting the ultimate ethical stakes between philosophy and tyranny.

#### 4. ORIG-274: Writing Manual → Newman's *The Idea of a University*
* **Course Assignment:** PHI 710 (*The Thesis Capstone*, Stage 700)
* **Conflict with Curricular Vision:** Vaughn's *Writing Philosophy* provides prescriptive guidelines for thesis formatting, citation hygiene, and paragraph pacing. In a culminating 700-level capstone seminar, students require deep intellectual contemplation on the purpose and unity of liberal knowledge rather than elementary writing drills.
* **Replacement Strategy:**
  - John Henry Newman's **The Idea of a University** (*ORIG-274*). Newman's monumental lectures define liberal knowledge as an end in itself (*Discourses V and VII*). He articulates why philosophical enlargement of mind, rather than narrow professional utility, constitutes the true aim of a university education.

## Section 2: Preserved Borderline & Scholarly Works: Scholarly Justification

An essential tenet of curriculum curation is that **difficulty, technicality, and specialized scholarship must not be confused with mechanical drill pedagogy**. The curriculum deliberately retains authoritative scholarly companions, comprehensive multi-author anthologies, and high-level historical commentaries. Below is the scholarly justification for each preserved category:

### 1. Classical Primary Sources with Challenging Structures (Strictly Preserved)
* **Moses Maimonides: *The Guide of the Perplexed (Selections)*** (`N78`, PHI 302):
  - *Status:* Preserved Primary Source (Spine).
  - *Justification:* Despite the word "Guide" in its title, this 12th-century philosophical masterpiece is one of the deepest syntheses of biblical revelation and Aristotelian metaphysics ever composed. It is an indispensable primary text for understanding the medieval reception of Aristotle and directly influenced Thomas Aquinas (`ORIG-058`).
* **Mary Wollstonecraft: *A Vindication of the Rights of Woman*** (`ORIG-145`, HIS 504):
  - *Status:* Preserved Primary Source (Spine).
  - *Justification:* A foundational Enlightenment political treatise applying republican and natural rights theory to the moral status of women, engaging directly with Rousseau, Locke, and Burke.

### 2. Comprehensive Scholarly Reference Handbooks (Strictly Preserved as Reference Lane)
* **The Oxford Handbooks Series** (`ORIG-040`, `ORIG-041`, `ORIG-232`, `ORIG-246`, `ORIG-249`, `ORIG-252`, `ORIG-267`, `ORIG-271`, `ORIG-273`):
  - *Status:* Preserved Reference Works (`lane: 'Reference'`, `type: 'C'`).
  - *Justification:* These handbooks are not pedagogical drill workbooks or textbooks. They are state-of-the-art scholarly reference compendia containing peer-reviewed essays by leading contemporary philosophers (e.g., Jonardon Ganeri, Jay Garfield, Jerrold Levinson, David Copp). They situate primary texts within current academic debates and historical lineages, serving as indispensable research tools for seminar papers and capstone inquiries.

### 3. Canonical Scholarly Commentaries & Companions (Strictly Preserved as Context Lane)
* **Paul Guyer: *Kant*** (`ORIG-132`, PHI 501):
  - *Status:* Preserved Scholarly Monograph (`lane: 'Context'`, `type: 'S'`).
  - *Justification:* Guyer is the preeminent contemporary translator and commentator on Immanuel Kant. His monograph provides rigorous conceptual scaffolding for the *Critique of Pure Reason* (`ORIG-133`), elucidating the transcendental deduction, the antinomies, and the distinction between phenomena and noumena without dumbing down the text.
* **Marc Bloch: *The Historian's Craft*** (`ORIG-004`, HIS 102):
  - *Status:* Preserved Historical Classic (`lane: 'Spine'`, `type: 'P'`).
  - *Justification:* Written by the co-founder of the Annales School while in French Resistance captivity, this is a profound meditation on historical evidence, involuntary testimony, and the nature of human time, light-years removed from a modern trade workbook.
* **Cambridge Companions Series** (Kraut on Plato `ORIG-028`, Barnes on Aristotle `ORIG-030`, Magnus & Higgins on Nietzsche `ORIG-136`, Fricker & Hornsby on Feminist Philosophy `ORIG-269`):
  - *Status:* Preserved Reference & Context (`lane: 'Reference' | 'Context'`).
  - *Justification:* Essential scholarly companions that trace textual reception, philological disputes, and philosophical interpretations across 2,500 years of intellectual history.
